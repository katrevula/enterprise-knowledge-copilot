# Code Of Conduct

## Overview

Employees are expected to act with integrity, respect, accountability, and professionalism. The code of conduct applies to workplace interactions, company systems, business travel, and work-related events.

## Respectful Workplace

Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, bullying, and abusive conduct are not tolerated. Employees should report concerns to their manager, HR, or the ethics reporting channel.

## Conflicts Of Interest

Employees must disclose personal, financial, or outside work relationships that could interfere with company responsibilities or decision-making.

## Confidentiality

Employees must protect confidential company, customer, and employee information. Confidential information should only be shared with authorized individuals who have a business need to know.

## Reporting Concerns

Employees may report conduct concerns to a manager, HR Business Partner, Legal, or the ethics hotline. Retaliation against employees who raise good-faith concerns is prohibited.
