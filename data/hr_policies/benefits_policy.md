# Benefits Policy

## Overview

The company provides benefits intended to support employee health, financial security, and wellbeing. Benefit availability may vary by country, employment type, and local law.

## Health Benefits

Eligible employees may enroll in medical, dental, and vision coverage during onboarding or annual open enrollment. Qualifying life events may allow mid-year changes.

## Retirement Benefits

Eligible employees may participate in the company retirement plan after meeting local eligibility requirements. Company matching contributions follow the plan document.

## Wellness Support

Employees may use the wellness reimbursement program for eligible fitness, mental health, and preventive wellbeing expenses. Reimbursement requests must include receipts and be submitted within 60 days of purchase.

## Questions And Escalation

Benefits questions should be sent to Benefits Support. Disputed eligibility decisions may be escalated to HR Benefits within 10 business days.
