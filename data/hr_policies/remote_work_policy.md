# Remote Work Policy

## Overview

Remote work is available for eligible roles when job responsibilities, security requirements, and team collaboration needs can be met.

## Eligibility

Managers determine remote work eligibility based on role requirements, performance expectations, and business needs. Some roles require onsite presence for equipment, customer, or security reasons.

## Work Expectations

Remote employees must maintain reliable internet, attend required meetings, protect company information, and be available during agreed working hours. Employees should keep calendars current and use approved collaboration tools.

## Security

Company data must be accessed only through approved devices and secure connections. Employees may not store confidential files on personal devices or unapproved cloud services.

## Changes

Remote arrangements may be reviewed if business needs, performance expectations, or security requirements change. Employees should request location changes before working from a new state or country.
