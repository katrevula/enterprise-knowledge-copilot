# Incident Escalation Policy

## Overview

This policy defines how employees report, escalate, and communicate operational incidents. Incidents include outages, security concerns, data exposure, workplace safety concerns, and business-critical process failures.

## Severity Levels

Severity 1 incidents are critical events that create broad service disruption, confirmed data exposure, or immediate safety risk. Severity 2 incidents create major workflow interruption for one or more teams. Severity 3 incidents are limited issues with a workaround.

## Escalation Process

Employees should report incidents in the service desk portal or designated incident channel. The incident owner acknowledges Severity 1 incidents within 15 minutes, Severity 2 incidents within 1 business hour, and Severity 3 incidents within 1 business day.

For Severity 1 incidents, the incident owner immediately notifies the duty manager, Security if applicable, HR if employee safety or conduct is involved, and Communications if broad employee messaging is needed.

## Communication

Incident updates should include current impact, mitigation steps, next update time, and owner. Severity 1 updates are posted every 30 minutes until stable. Severity 2 updates are posted every 2 business hours.

## Post-Incident Review

Severity 1 and Severity 2 incidents require a post-incident review within 5 business days. The review documents root cause, timeline, corrective actions, and accountable owners.
