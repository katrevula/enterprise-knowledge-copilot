# Leave Policy

## Overview

Employees receive paid time off to support rest, illness, caregiving, civic duty, and major life events. Leave requests should be submitted in the HR portal when the need is known in advance.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave should be requested at least 10 business days before the planned absence. Managers approve requests based on team coverage and business continuity.

Unused annual leave can carry over up to 5 days into the next calendar year. Carryover days must be used by March 31 unless an exception is approved by HR.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave can be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or caring for an immediate family member.

If sick leave lasts more than 3 consecutive business days, HR may request supporting documentation. Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

## Family And Parental Leave

Eligible employees may request up to 12 weeks of job-protected family leave for birth, adoption, serious health conditions, or caregiving needs. Paid portions depend on location and applicable law.

## Emergency Leave

Emergency leave may be granted for unexpected events such as bereavement, natural disaster, or urgent family care. Employees should contact their manager and HR as soon as practical.

## Escalation

If a leave request is denied and the employee believes the decision conflicts with policy, the employee should first discuss the issue with the manager. If unresolved, the employee may escalate to HR Operations within 5 business days.
