# IT Access Policy

## Overview

IT access is granted based on role, least privilege, and manager approval. Access is reviewed periodically to protect company systems and data.

## Requesting Access

Employees request new access through the service desk portal. Requests must include the business reason, system name, requested role, and manager approval.

## New Hire Access

Standard new hire access is provisioned before Day One when HR profile creation is complete. Role-specific access is requested by the hiring manager.

## Access Issues

Employees who cannot access required systems should open a service desk ticket. Critical access blockers for customer-impacting work should be marked urgent and escalated to the duty manager.

## Termination And Transfers

Access is removed or updated when employees leave the company, change roles, or transfer teams. Managers are responsible for confirming access changes during role transitions.
