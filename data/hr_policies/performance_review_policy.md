# Performance Review Policy

## Overview

Performance reviews align employee goals, feedback, development, and business outcomes. Managers should provide ongoing feedback throughout the year.

## Goal Setting

Employees and managers set goals during the planning cycle. Goals should be specific, measurable, relevant to the role, and reviewed regularly.

## Midyear Check-In

The midyear check-in focuses on progress, blockers, feedback, and goal adjustments. It is not a final rating event.

## Annual Review

The annual review summarizes outcomes, behaviors, strengths, development areas, and future priorities. Managers calibrate ratings according to company guidance.

## Development Plans

When performance gaps are identified, managers should provide clear expectations, support, timelines, and regular follow-up. HR should be consulted for formal performance improvement plans.
