# Employee Onboarding Policy

## Overview

The onboarding process helps new employees become productive, secure, and connected during their first 30 days.

## Before Day One

Recruiting sends the signed offer packet to HR Operations. HR Operations creates the employee profile, confirms work location, and triggers IT account provisioning.

The hiring manager prepares the first-week plan, assigns an onboarding buddy, and confirms required equipment.

## Day One

New employees attend orientation, complete required compliance forms, review the code of conduct, and confirm access to core systems. The manager reviews role expectations and the first-week schedule.

## First Week

The employee completes security awareness training, role-specific onboarding, and team introductions. IT access issues should be submitted through the service desk.

## First 30 Days

The manager schedules a 30-day check-in to review progress, clarify goals, and collect onboarding feedback. HR reviews completion of required forms and trainings.

## Escalation

Blocked onboarding items should first go to the hiring manager. System access blockers should go to IT Support. Compliance or document issues should go to HR Operations.
